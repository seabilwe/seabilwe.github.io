Where the World Lives, 2025

Population density at 1 km resolution, from the Global Human Settlement Layer. Zoom in to see how people cluster along rivers, coasts and city corridors.

Global population density

Each cell counts the people living within a square kilometre. The darkest cells are the dense cores of cities; the pale ground between them is where almost no one lives.

Source: Global Human Settlement Layer (GHS-POP), European Commission JRC. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
