Zimbabwe's eleven national parks

The national parks set against the rest of Zimbabwe's protected estate, its rivers and relief, and the roads and towns around it. A 2023 map, redesigned and made interactive in 2026.

What the map shows

  • 6.9%of Zimbabwe, 26,866 km², is national park.
  • 28%is protected once wildlife management areas, safari areas, forests and recreation parks are added.
  • 55%of all national park land lies in a single park, Hwange.

Explore the map

Switch between the four themes. Each one explains what it shows; click a park or protected area for its details.

The finished QGIS map, ready to print. Click it to view it full screen, or download it.

How it was made

  • Parks and the wider protected estate from the World Database on Protected Areas; World Heritage overlaps removed so no land is counted twice.
  • Areas measured in an Albers equal-area projection centred on Zimbabwe (standard parallels 16.5°S and 21.5°S), replacing UTM 36S, which skewed the west of the country.
  • Data fixes: a duplicated, misplaced “Karoi” town point removed, Matopos labelled by its official name, Matobo, and Lake Kariba drawn whole across the border.
  • Labels placed directly on the parks with leader lines to the small ones; neighbouring countries faded and named; relief kept to Zimbabwe.

Data: WDPA, August 2023 (UNEP-WCMC and IUCN) · Boundaries: ZimGeospatial · Towns, roads, railways, rivers and water bodies: Humanitarian Data Exchange and OpenStreetMap via Geofabrik, © OpenStreetMap contributors · Relief: Esri World Shaded Relief · Neighbours and lakes: Natural Earth.
